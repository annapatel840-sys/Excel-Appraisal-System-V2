Appraisal Cycle screen (as in Appraisal_Cycle_v15)

The cycle grid, set up, status line, steps and checks are the Appraisal Cycle screen as built. They are fixed in the screen, not configured here. Demo data.

Not set here: budget formula (always Hike Amount + PB + RB + Joining Bonus), rating scale (comes from the feedback upload) and rating rounding (none). Business rules such as the PB and RB preload stay in the tool.
